## Purpose

Record the branding assets currently in use and the logo directions under consideration. The definitive mark is still being researched; this record keeps the working decision explicit.

## Current selection

The **serif monogram "MS"** is the working logo for the project's start:

- `docs/assets/logo.png` — current logo mark (256×512 source: `logo-concept-B-monogram.png`).

Rationale: it matches the editorial typography direction of the Hi-Fi Editorial Station (see `docs/standards/DesignSystem.md` §3), reads cleanly at small sizes, and leaves room to evolve into a dedicated symbol later.
